About

"Setting trends from DC to NYC. I don't just create beats; I build movements."

Tony Brown, professionally known as MZPRD (Meztheprod), is an American Music Producer & Entertainment Entrepreneur from Southeast Washington, D.C., now based in Pennsylvania. He is the Founder of RapAechelon and CEO of Genie Interactive Games — bridging the gap between indie talent and industry legends.

With nearly two decades in the industry, his production work spans respected names across hip-hop, blending raw authenticity with a modern sheen. His philosophy is simple: making the truth sound good.

Journey

From Southeast DC to the industry.

Roots

Southeast Washington, D.C.

Born and raised in Southeast D.C., Tony grew up around hip-hop — taking the music more seriously than ever once he was on his own, flipping soul samples and studying the greats.

2005

Relocation to Pennsylvania

Moved to Pennsylvania and began working with top local and industry hip-hop artists as a producer, DJ, and radio personality — building a reputation across the East Coast scene.

Career

Nearly two decades of production

Production and engineering work with artists including G Dep, French Montana, Peedi Crakk, Red Mcfly, Twista, Coke Boys, Styles P, Stro the MC, and Lil Flip — featured across XXL, Genius, and 2dopeboyz.

2024

YN Bounce is born

Coined YN Bounce — a new genre fusing chopped soul loops with high-tempo bounce built for the club and the streets.

Now

RapAechelon · Genie Interactive Games

Founder of RapAechelon and CEO of Genie Interactive Games — plus the Steam horror title The Cecil: The Journey Begins and the Criminal Case Network channel. Setting trends from DC to NYC.
