About

"Setting trends from DC to NYC. I don't just create beats; I build movements."

Tony Brown, professionally known as MZPRD (Meztheprod), is a Music Producer & Entertainment Entrepreneur. He is the Founder of @RapAechelon and CEO of Genie Interactive Games — bridging the gap between indie talent and industry legends.

As a producer, his credits include Red Mcfly, French Montana, Twista, Stro, Peedi Crakk, and Lil Flip. His work blends raw authenticity with a modern sheen — making the truth sound good.
